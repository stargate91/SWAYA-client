# SWAYA Dashboard & Discovery Guide

Welcome to the **Dashboard**! Think of the Dashboard as your personalized home theater marquee. It brings together everything you're currently watching, newly added media in your local library, and endless recommendations tailored to your taste across the web.

---

## 1. Dual Mode Support (SFW & NSFW)

Just like the rest of SWAYA, your Dashboard seamlessly adapts to your active session mode:

* **Mainstream Mode (SFW):** Focuses on Hollywood movies, TV series, mainstream performers, and major film studios.
* **Adult Mode (NSFW):** Switches your feed to adult scenes, adult movies, performers, and specialty adult studios.

> [!NOTE]
> Switching modes in the top navigation bar instantly morphs the entire Dashboard in real time without refreshing the page!

---

## 2. Connected Providers & API Keys

Your recommendations are powered by the provider integrations configured in **Settings**:
* **TMDb (The Movie Database):** Drives mainstream movie/TV suggestions, trending lists, and top genre discoveries.
* **StashDB & FansDB:** Powers deep adult scene discovery, performer lookups, and trending adult releases.

*If an API key is not configured for a specific service, that service's discovery widget will simply sleep quietly in the background.*

---

## 3. Customize Your Layout (Your Dashboard, Your Rules)

You have total control over what appears on your home screen:
* **Show or Hide Any Widget:** Don't care about a specific feed? Toggle it off in your dashboard layout settings.
* **Drag-and-Drop Reordering:** Move your favorite widgets to the top (e.g. put *Continue Watching* right under the banner or move *Recently Added* to the forefront).
* **Instant Persistence:** SWAYA saves your layout automatically so it always looks exactly the way you left it.

---

## 4. Spotlight Banner (The Hero Feature)

At the top of your dashboard sits the **Spotlight Banner**: a full-width hero presentation highlighting the hottest recommendation of the day.

### Highlights
* **Backdrop Art & Details:** Displays crystal-clear high-res backdrop art, rating badges (TMDb / IMDb), release date, and plot synopsis.
* **1-Click Watchlist:** Add the highlighted title directly to your Watchlist with a single tap.
* **Provider Switcher (Adult Mode):** In NSFW mode, toggle between StashDB, FansDB, and TMDb Adult recommendations right from the banner pill controls.

---

## 5. Continue Watching (Pick Up Right Where You Left Off)

Never lose your spot in a movie or TV marathon.
* **Live Progress Bar:** Displays your exact progress percentage and time left (e.g. `45% · 32m left`).
* **Episode Context:** For TV shows, clearly displays the season and episode code (e.g., `S02E05`).
* **1-Click Play Overlay:** Hover over any card and hit the glowing Play button to resume playback instantly.
* **Remove Progress:** Finished or changed your mind? Hover and tap the red minus button in the top-right corner to clear it from your active queue.

---

## 6. Recently Added to Library

Keep tabs on your latest imports across all media types:
* **Movies & TV Shows:** Recently scanned feature films and series.
* **Scenes & Videos:** New standalone video clips and adult scenes.
* **Stars & Studios:** Newly followed actors, performers, and production companies.

---

## 7. Mainstream Recommendations (SFW Mode)

Discover your next favorite watch with dedicated curated feeds:

### 1. TMDb Movie & TV Recommendations
Smart suggestions generated based on trending popularity and what's currently buzzing.

### 2. Top 20 Discoveries (Interactive Filters)
Dive deep into cinema history with interactive drop-downs:
* **Filter by Genre:** Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi, Horror, Drama, Animation, and more.
* **Filter by Year:** From classic decades to this year's latest blockbusters.

---

## 8. Adult Scene Discovery (NSFW Mode)

In NSFW mode, your discovery feeds unlock deep studio and scene archives:

### StashDB & FansDB Feeds
* **Dynamic Sorting:** Browse by **Trending**, **Popularity**, or **Latest Additions**.
* **Tag & Performer Focus:** Explore scenes categorized by tags and studios.
* **Infinite Scroll:** Scroll horizontally as far as you want-SWAYA will keep loading more discoveries smoothly without interruption.
* **1-Click Watchlist:** Spot a scene you want to download or track? Hit the **Watchlist** button on the card to save it immediately.
