# SWAYA Detail Pages Guide

Welcome to the **Detail Pages**! When you click on any movie, TV show, scene, actor, or studio in SWAYA, you open its dedicated detail page. Think of a detail page as a rich encyclopedia profile packed with information, artwork, playback controls, and discovery tools.

---

## 1. Dual Mode Support (SFW & NSFW)

Detail pages automatically adjust to your active session mode:

* **Mainstream Mode (SFW):** Focuses on movies, TV series, actors, directors, box office numbers, and Hollywood studios.
* **Adult Mode (NSFW):** Focuses on scenes, adult movies, performers, physical traits, studio networks, and finish snapshot galleries.

---

## 2. Media Detail Pages (Movies, TV Shows & Scenes)

Every media item has a full-screen profile that puts you in total control of its presentation and playback.

### Hero Banner & Artwork Pickers
* **Cinematic Backdrop:** A full-width backdrop image welcomes you at the top of the page.
* **Backdrop Picker:** Click the image icon in the top utility bar to choose from multiple high-resolution online backdrops or upload your own picture.
* **Logo Picker:** Click directly on the title or logo graphic to swap it for alternate transparent PNG logos from TMDb or community sources.
* **Poster Picker:** If an item uses a poster layout or is missing artwork, click the poster to pick alternative cover art.
* **Live Video Preview (Scenes):** Hover or click the center play button in the hero banner to stream a seamless, looped video preview directly inside the header.

### Header Badges & Quick Info
* **Metadata Pills:** Quick badges showing release date, runtime duration, age rating, spoken languages, and studio or network names.
* **Ratings:** Integrated score badges for IMDb, TMDb, and ThePornDB.
* **Personal Star Rating:** Give the title your own score from 1 to 10 stars with a single click.
* **Plot Synopsis:** Read the full storyline. For longer stories, click the summary to open an expanded reading drawer.

### Action Buttons
The bottom action bar gives you instant one-click controls:
* **Play / Resume:** Launches the video in the built-in media player. If you were in the middle of watching, it resumes right where you stopped. For TV shows, it automatically queues up your next unwatched episode.
* **Watchlist:** Save the title to your quick Watchlist with one tap.
* **Watched / Unwatched Toggle:** Flip the watched status at any time.
* **Custom Lists:** Click the Lists button to add or remove the title from any of your custom collections.
* **Track / Untrack:** For titles you don't own yet, toggle tracking to keep them monitored in your library.
* **Trailer:** Watch official YouTube trailers inside a clean video overlay.
* **Collection Shortcut:** Jump straight to the complete movie franchise or series page.
* **Torrent & Download:** If torrent integration is enabled, open a 1-click torrent search modal to find and download the release.
* **Add Peak (Adult Mode):** Mark a favorite moment or climax timestamp in the video.
* **Technical Details Drawer:** View file formats, video codecs, audio channels, bitrate, and exact hard drive file paths.
* **Delete Item:** Choose whether to remove the item from your SWAYA database only or send the physical files on your disk to the Recycle Bin / Trash.

### In-Depth Content Sections
* **Cast & Performers:** Clickable photo cards of everyone who starred in or directed the title.
* **Seasons & Episodes (TV Shows):** An interactive accordion breakdown of every season and episode, complete with episode descriptions, thumbnails, and individual play buttons.
* **Box Office & Budget (Movies):** View production budgets and worldwide box office earnings.
* **Production Studios & Networks:** Clickable badges linking to the studios that created the media.
* **Tags Manager:** Add custom color-coded tags to group and categorize your library.
* **Watch Statistics:** See your total play count, watch history, and timestamps.
* **Finish Snapshots & Peaks (Adult Mode):** A visual list of captured climax moments. Click any timestamp to jump straight to that exact scene moment.

## 3. People & Performer Detail Pages

Clicking on any actor, director, or performer opens their comprehensive artist profile.

### Profile Header & Biography
* **Headshot & Backdrop:** Displays high-res portrait art and scenic backdrops, complete with one-click image switchers.
* **Biography:** Read their life story and career background.
* **Physical Traits & Attributes (Adult Mode):** Details such as birthdate, age, birthplace, height, weight, ethnicity, hair color, eye color, body measurements, tattoos, and piercings.
* **Favorite & Follow Toggle:** Mark performers as favorites or activate/deactivate tracking.
* **Social Links:** Direct shortcuts to Instagram, Twitter / X, official websites, IMDb, TMDb, or FreeOnes.

### Filmography & Content Views
* **In Library:** Browse only the movies, TV shows, and scenes starring this person that you already have downloaded on your computer.
* **Live Discovery (Full Filmography):** Browse their entire real-world filmography fetched from TMDb, StashDB, FansDB, or ThePornDB. Filter their credits by studio or tag, and download missing titles directly with torrent search.
* **Finish Snapshots (Adult Mode):** A photo gallery of every favorite moment snapshot captured during playback. Click any photo to play that exact moment in the video player.

---

## 4. Studio Detail Pages

Clicking on any studio or television network opens its production hub.

### Studio Hub Features
* **Brand Logo & Backdrop:** High-resolution studio branding with built-in logo and backdrop pickers.
* **Studio Relations:** View parent companies, child sub-studios, and broadcast networks.
* **Official Links & Rating:** Quick access to official websites and your personal studio rating.

### Content Views
* **In Library:** View all movies, TV shows, or scenes made by this studio that currently live on your local drive.
* **Live Discovery:** Explore the studio's full release catalog across online databases with custom sorting and filters.
