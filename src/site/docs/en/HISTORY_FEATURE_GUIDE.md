# SWAYA History & Time Machine Guide

Welcome to the **History** page! Think of this page as your personal time machine and activity log in SWAYA. It keeps track of everything important that happens behind the scenes: when you organize and rename your media files, what movies or TV episodes you've watched, and in adult mode, the exact finish moments you've bookmarked.

---

## 1. Rename & Organization History (Your Safety Net)

Whenever you import files or run the **Organizer** to rename and structure your library, SWAYA creates a detailed batch log here. You never have to worry about accidentally organizing the wrong folder.

### What You See on Each Card
* **Batch Summary:** See the date and time of the batch, its unique ID, and a breakdown of what was processed (e.g. `2 Movies`, `14 Episodes`, `3 Extras`).
* **Success & Failure Counts:** At a glance badges show how many files succeeded, failed, or were already reverted.
* **Detailed File Log:** Click **Show Details** on any batch to expand the full audit trail. You'll see the exact old folder path $\rightarrow$ new destination folder path, plus the exact old filename $\rightarrow$ new standardized filename.

### One-Click Revert (The Magic Undo Button)
Need to take back an organization batch? No problem:
1. Click the **Revert** button on any batch.
2. Confirm the action in the safety dialog.
3. SWAYA will physically move and rename all files back to their original folder locations and original names.
4. **Instant Return to Organizer:** All reverted items are automatically restored to the **Organizer page in `matched` status**, ready for you to re-review, adjust metadata, or organize again on your terms.

## 2. Playback & Watched History

The **Playback Logs** tab gives you a chronological feed of everything you've played in SWAYA across your entire library.

### Key Features
* **Live Progress & Status:**
  * **Watched:** Items you've finished show a clean green *Watched* badge.
  * **In Progress:** Shows the exact resume timestamp, duration, and a progress bar (e.g. `45% (42:15 / 1:34:00)`).
  * **Currently Playing:** If a video is actively playing right now, you'll see a live pulsing indicator.
* **One-Click Play & Resume:**
  * Click **Continue** to resume an in-progress video right where you left off.
  * Click **Rewatch** to jump back into a previously completed movie or episode.
* **Instant Navigation:** Click any movie, TV show, or episode title to jump directly to its library detail page.

### Dual Mode Privacy (SFW vs. NSFW)
SWAYA respects your privacy and session mode:
* **In SFW Mode (Mainstream):** NSFW scenes and adult movies are cleanly and safely filtered out of your watched history.
* **In NSFW Mode (Adult):** The history view seamlessly displays your full playback activity across both mainstream and adult media.

---

## 3. Finish Logs (NSFW)

When you are in **NSFW Mode**, a special **Finish Logs** tab becomes available in the top bar.

> [!NOTE]
> In SFW mode, this tab is completely hidden and guarded to ensure absolute discretion.

### What are Finish Logs?
During adult video playback, you can tap the **Finish** button at any special moment of a scene. SWAYA immediately captures:
1. **The Exact Timestamp:** Saved down to the second (e.g., `Finish at 24:18`).
2. **High-Resolution Snapshot:** A crystal-clear frame capture taken directly from the video at that precise instant.
3. **Creation Time:** When the moment was recorded.

### Interacting with Finish Moments
* **Snapshot Lightbox:** Click on any snapshot thumbnail to open a full-screen, high-definition lightbox viewer.
* **Play Moment:** Click the **Play Moment** button on any card to immediately launch the player and seek directly to that exact second in the video.
* **Title Routing:** Click the scene or video title to view its full details, performer credits, tags, and related studio clips.
