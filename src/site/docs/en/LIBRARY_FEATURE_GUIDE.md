# SWAYA Library Guide

Welcome to the **Library**! Think of the Library as your personal digital bookshelf. It keeps all your movies, TV shows, clips, favorite actors, and studios in one place so you can browse, sort, and watch them whenever you want.

---

## 1. Dual Mode Support (SFW & NSFW)

Your Library works in two separate modes to keep your media neatly separated:

* **Mainstream Mode (SFW):** Shows all your family-friendly movies, TV series, home videos, mainstream actors, and movie studios.
* **Adult Mode (NSFW):** Switches to adult scenes, full-length adult movies, adult performers (stars), and specialty adult studios.

When you switch modes in the top navigation bar, your Library updates immediately to display only the content belonging to that mode.

---

## 2. Library Tabs

The top tab bar lets you jump straight to the exact type of media you want to see:

* **Movies:** Feature-length films.
* **TV Shows:** TV series with seasons and episode breakdowns.
* **Collections:** Movie series and themed movie franchises grouped together in one place.
* **Stars:** The actors, actresses, and performers who appear in your media.
* **Scenes:** Individual video clips, web episodes, or single adult scene releases.
* **Videos:** Standalone video files, personal recordings, and extras.
* **Studios:** The production houses and studios that created the content.
* **Tags:** Custom color-coded labels and thematic categories that span across your entire library.

---

## 3. Local vs. Tracked Media

Every media item in your Library has a status indicator that shows whether it lives on your computer or is just being tracked:

* **Local:** The video file is physically stored on your hard drive. You can click play and watch it right away with zero waiting.
* **Tracked:** You have saved the title and its metadata in your library to keep track of it, but the actual video file is not stored on your local drive yet.

---

## 4. Searching, Filtering, and Sorting

You can find exactly what you want using the top tool bar on every tab.

### Media Items (Movies, TV Shows, Scenes, Videos)

#### Sorters
* **Title:** Alphabetical order from A to Z or Z to A.
* **Release Date / First Air Date:** Sort from the newest releases to older classics.
* **Last Air Date:** (TV Shows) Sort by when the latest episode aired.
* **Seasons / Episodes Count:** (TV Shows) Sort by how many seasons or episodes the show has.
* **Ratings:** Sort by TMDb Rating, IMDb Rating, or your personal User Rating.
* **Duration:** Sort by how long or short the video is.
* **File Size:** (Local files) Sort by how much disk space the video takes up.
* **Watch Activity:** Sort by Last Watched, Watch Count, or Last Finish / Finish Count (Adult Mode).
* **Tag Count:** Sort by how many tags are attached to the item.
* **Random:** Shuffle your items into a random order for a surprise pick.

#### Filters
* **Search:** Type any title, performer name, studio name, or keyword.
* **Ownership Status:** Switch between viewing Local files (on disk), Tracked items (bookmarks), or All.
* **Watch Status:** Filter by Watched, Unwatched, or All.
* **Genres:** Filter by movie or show genres (Action, Comedy, Drama, Sci-Fi, etc.).
* **Decade & Year:** Toggle between Decade mode (e.g. 2020s, 2010s, 1990s) or pick an exact release year.
* **Tags:** Select one or multiple colored tags at the same time.
* **Performer:** Select a specific actor or star to see only their titles.
* **Studio & Network:** Filter by production studio, film company, or broadcast network (e.g. HBO, Netflix).

---

### Stars & People

#### Sorters
* **Name:** Alphabetical order from A to Z or Z to A.
* **Popularity & Rating:** Sort by popularity or online performer ratings.
* **User Rating:** Sort by your personal star rating.
* **Library Count:** Sort by who has the most videos inside your collection.
* **Birthdate:** Sort by age and date of birth.
* **Watch & Finish Activity:** Sort by Last Watched, Watch Count, Last Finish, or Finish Count.
* **Height & Weight:** Sort performers by physical dimensions.
* **Measurements & Body Types (Adult Mode):** Sort by breast / cup size (A to K scale), waist size, hip size, hourglass ratio, slender / athletic build, or curvy build.
* **Tag Count & Random:** Sort by tag count or shuffle randomly.

#### Filters
* **Search:** Search performers by name.
* **Favourite:** Quickly view only your starred favorite performers.
* **Role (Mainstream):** Filter by Actor, Director, Writer, or Composer.
* **Gender:** Filter by Female, Male, or All.
* **Ethnicity & Hair Color:** Filter by ethnicity and hair color.
* **Advanced Physical Attributes (Adult Mode):** Filter by eye color, breast type (natural or augmented), breast size (small, medium, big, extra big), butt shape (bubble, heart, square, flat), butt size, tattoos (yes/no), and piercings (yes/no).

---

### Studios

* **Sorters:** Studio Name (A-Z), Library Count, User Rating.
* **Filters:** Search by name, Favourite toggle, and Type filter (Companies vs. Networks in Mainstream Mode).

---

### Collections

* **Sorters:** Title (A-Z) and Item Count (number of movies owned in the collection).
* **Filters:** Search by name and Collection Status (All, Complete, or In Progress).

---

### Tags

* **Sorters:** Name (A-Z) and Total Item Count (how many items in your library share this tag).
* **Filters:** Real-time text search to filter your tag collection.

## 5. Tags & Tag Creation

Tags help you organize and categorize your library however you want. You can create new tags or edit existing ones at any time:

* **Custom Color Palette:** Pick any custom color or choose from predefined theme swatches (accent blue, success green, warning orange, danger red) to color-code your tag cards and pills.
* **Custom Artwork & Focal Point:** Upload up to 3 custom cover images or paste image URLs. You can drag the focal point crosshairs to position the thumbnail framing perfectly.
* **Predefined Artwork & Looped Video Previews (Adult Mode):**
  * When creating tags in Adult Mode, SWAYA connects to online tag databases (such as Feederbox and StashDB).
  * As you type a tag name, smart suggestions appear showing matching community tags.
  * You can select pre-built static illustration badges or animated looped video clip previews. SWAYA automatically downloads and caches these assets locally so your tag grid looks lively and dynamic.
* **Interactive Tag Filter:** Clicking any tag inside your Library opens all movies, series, scenes, videos, and stars matching that exact tag.

---

## 6. Adding Stars and Studios

Want to follow your favorite actors or studios? Click the **Add Person** or **Add Studio** buttons at the top of their respective tabs:

* **Smart Suggestions:** The modal automatically suggests people and studios that already appear inside your local media files.
* **Complete Database Search:** You are not limited to suggestions. You can search the full connected database (like TMDb, StashDB, or FansDB) to find and track any person or studio, even if you do not have their files on your drive yet.

---

## 7. Poster Card Quick Actions

Every card in your Library grid comes with handy action buttons right on the card:

* **Play Button:** Launches the video instantly in SWAYA's built-in player.
* **Watch / Unwatch Button:** Mark a title as watched or unwatched with a single click.
* **Poster Switcher Button:** Open the artwork picker to choose alternate posters and cover art for that item.
