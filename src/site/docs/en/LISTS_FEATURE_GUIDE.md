# SWAYA Custom Lists Guide

Custom Lists give you full control over how you organize, group, and track your media. Whether you want to build a weekend movie marathon, save recommendations from friends, track a specific actor's filmography, or keep your personal favorites together, Custom Lists make curation fast and flexible.

---

## 1. Dual Mode Support (SFW & NSFW)

SWAYA completely separates your mainstream and adult collections into two independent environments:

* **Mainstream Mode (SFW):** Manage your standard **Movies & TV Shows**, standalone **Videos**, and **People** (actors, directors, crew).
* **Adult Mode (NSFW):** Manage your **Adult Movies & TV**, **Videos & Scenes**, and **Adult Stars**.

When you switch session modes, the sidebar and header instantly display only the lists relevant to that mode, preventing any accidental cross-contamination of content.

---

## 2. List Types

When creating a new list, you choose a dedicated type to tailor the list's capabilities:

| List Type | Mode | What You Can Store |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Movies & TV** | SFW & NSFW | Feature films and television series |
| **Videos** *(or Videos & Scenes in NSFW)* | SFW & NSFW | Standalone video files, home videos, and individual adult scenes |
| **People** | SFW & NSFW | Actors, performers, directors, writers, and crew members |

---

## 3. How to Add Items to Your Lists

You can add items to your lists from anywhere in the application:

### 1. From the Lists Page (Local & Online Search)
Inside any list, click the **Add Items** button in the header. A search window opens where you can:
* Search your **Local Library** for titles already in your collection.
* Search **Online Databases** (such as TMDb, ThePornDB, StashDB, and FansDB) to save items you do not own yet.

### 2. From Media Detail Pages
On any movie, show, episode, scene, or person detail page, you can manage list assignments in two convenient places:
* Click the **Add to List** action button in the hero header.
* Use the list management action in the **Top Utility Bar** in the upper-right corner as you scroll through the page.

### 3. From Dashboard & Poster Cards
Hover over any card on the Dashboard, Library grid, or Recommendations carousel and use the quick action menu to add that item directly to a list.

## 4. Customizing Your Lists

Make each list stand out with personalized visual identity:

* **Theme Color:** Pick a color from the preset palette. This color accents the header banner, glow highlights, active filters, and sidebar badges for that list.
* **Cover Collage vs. Custom Image:**
  * **Dynamic Collage:** By default, SWAYA generates a clean, multi-poster collage from the first four items in your list.
  * **Custom Cover Upload:** Click the cover thumbnail in the header to upload any custom image file from your computer. You can reset back to the default collage at any time.
* **Name & Description:** Provide clear titles and notes to document what the list is for.

---

## 5. Filtering and Sorting Within a List

Every list includes a sticky top filter bar so you can find items instantly, even in lists with hundreds of entries:

* **Search:** Filter items by typing titles, actor names, or keywords.
* **Library Status Filter:** Filter between items you already have on your hard drive versus items that are only saved as bookmarks.
* **Watched Status Filter:** Filter between watched and unwatched titles.
* **Type Filter:** Switch between Movies and TV Shows (or Scenes and Videos).
* **Genre Filter:** Filter titles by specific genres.
* **People Filters:** In People lists, filter performers by gender and role (Actor, Director, Writer, Composer).
* **Sorting:** Sort by Date Added, Title, Release Date, User Rating, or Community Rating in ascending or descending order.

---

## 6. Backup & Portability: Export and Import

Your lists are portable and easy to share or restore:

* **Export List:** Click the **Export** button in the list header to download a clean `.json` file containing all list metadata and items.
* **Import List:** Click the **Import** button in the sidebar to upload an exported `.json` file. SWAYA will automatically create the list, resolve any name duplicates, and populate its items.
