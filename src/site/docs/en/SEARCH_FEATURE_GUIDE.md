# SWAYA Search & Discovery Guide

Finding movies, TV shows, actors, and scenes in SWAYA is super fast and easy. This guide walks you through how searching works, how safe mode protects your screen, and what you can find across different sites.

---

## How Search Works

There are two easy ways to search in SWAYA:

```
                      [ Type what you're looking for ]
                                     │
                 ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
                 ▼                                       ▼
     ┌───────────────────────┐               ┌───────────────────────┐
     │   Top Bar Search      │               │   Full Search Page    │
     │   (Quick & Easy)      │               │   (Big Grid & Filters)│
     ├───────────────────────┤               ├───────────────────────┤
     │ • Instant suggestions │               │ • Big poster cards    │
     │ • Keyboard friendly   │               │ • Source filter tabs  │
     │ • Quick jump to items │               │ • Category buttons    │
     └───────────────────────┘               └───────────────────────┘
```

1. **Top Bar Quick Search:**
   * Click the search bar at the very top of the window and start typing.
   * Instant suggestions pop up right below it in a tidy preview list.
   * **3 Ways to Jump to the Full Search Page:**
     * **Click the little pop-out icon on the right:** Takes you straight to the full search page (even when the search bar is empty, ready for browsing).
     * **Press Enter:** Instantly opens the full search page with your typed keywords.
     * **Click "See all results..." at the bottom of the list:** Opens the big page if you want to explore beyond the top suggestions.
   * Use the **Arrow keys** to move up and down through suggestions, or press **Escape** to dismiss.

2. **Full Search Page:**
   * Perfect when you want to browse lots of results on a big, comfortable screen with rich posters.
   * Switch between different websites (like TMDb or StashDB) with one click.
   * Filter by Movies, TV Shows, Stars, or Studios.
   * Hit the **Load More Results (+20)** button at the bottom whenever you want to keep exploring.

## Safe Mode & Adult Content (Keeping Things Private)

SWAYA makes sure you never see anything you don't want to see by mistake.

```
                  Is "Adult Content" turned ON in Settings?
                                ╱        ╲
                            NO ╱          ╲ YES
                              ╱            ╲
                             ▼              ▼
                 [ Family Friendly ]     Which mode are you in right now?
                 • No adult results                ╱        ╲
                 • Only mainstream TV & Movies SFW╱          ╲ NSFW
                                                 ╱            ╲
                                                ▼              ▼
                                       [ Blurred Covers ]   [ Clear Covers ]
                                       • Pictures blurred   • Full pictures
                                       • Safe from peeking  • Ready to play
```

### How the 3 Safety Levels Work:

1. **Adult Content is Turned OFF in Settings (Family-Friendly):**
   * SWAYA only searches mainstream movies and shows.
   * No 18+ content or adult websites will ever appear in your search results.

2. **Adult Content is ON, but you are in SFW Mode (Flame icon off):**
   * You can still search for adult scenes and stars, but **all preview images and covers are automatically blurred out** with a dark shield.
   * You won't accidentally show anything sensitive to people sitting nearby.

3. **Adult Content is ON, and you are in NSFW Mode (Flame icon active):**
   * All picture covers, backdrops, and actor photos are shown in full clarity.

## Where SWAYA Searches (Sources & Types)

You can pick which website SWAYA searches on using the **Source** and **Type** buttons at the top of the search page:

| Website | What You Can Search For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **TMDb** *(Mainstream)* | All (Everything at once), Movies, TV Shows, Stars (Actors & Actresses), Collections, Studios |
| **StashDB** | Scenes (Video clips), Adult Stars, Studios |
| **FansDB** | Scenes (Video clips), Adult Stars, Studios |
| **ThePornDB** | Scenes (Video clips), Movies (Full), Adult Stars, Studios |

### What Each Category Means in Simple Terms:

* **All:** Searches across movies, TV series, actors, and collections all at the same time.
* **Movies:** Finds feature films, animated movies, and cinema releases with cover art, release year, and duration.
* **TV Shows:** Finds full series, season counts, and air dates.
* **Stars / Adult Stars:** Finds actors, actresses, and performers with their photo profiles, bios, and what they played in.
* **Collections:** Finds movie franchises grouped together (like *Harry Potter*, *Star Wars*, or *The Lord of the Rings*).
* **Studios:** Finds production houses and networks (like *Disney*, *Warner Bros*, *HBO*, *Brazzers*, or *Blacked*).
* **Scenes:** Finds individual video clips and scenes with studio names, dates, and performer lists.

## 1-Minute Setup: Connecting Your Accounts

To search online databases, SWAYA just needs your free API keys:

1. **For Mainstream Movies & TV:**
   * Go to **Settings > API Keys**.
   * Paste your free **TMDb API Key** (and optional OMDb key).
2. **For Adult Scenes & Performers:**
   * Go to **Settings > Adult Content**.
   * Paste your **StashDB**, **FansDB**, or **ThePornDB** API keys.
3. **Your Keys Stay on Your Computer:**
   * SWAYA talks directly to these websites from your computer. Your keys and search history are never shared or sent to any third-party cloud servers.
